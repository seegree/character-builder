What changed

  • Spray-shade texture. Each form gets a soft shadow + highlight (feathered, clipped to the shape); grain then rides those gradients — the reason flat grain did nothing was that there were no gradients to grip. Now it reads gritty and dimensional.
  • Eyes: smaller, rounder solid ovals with catchlights — and the mirror bug that made one eye a “water balloon” is gone.
  • Legs: tapered thigh→ankle with a gentle organic bow, instead of stiff rectangles.
  • Shoes: proportional, the leg enters the ankle, toe cap + laces read.

The texture system, in short

Form

Per shape: a shadow (multiply) and highlight (screen), soft-feathered and clipped to the silhouette. Light reads from upper-left.

Grit

A grain pass over everything. It’s invisible on flat color but bites on the shadow/highlight gradients — that’s the gritty riso/airbrush feel.

Separable

Shading + grit are their own layers, so the whole texture is a dial we can push per style, or strip for a clean flat look.
