How it's built

Art-agnostic rigger

A standalone CBRIG.rig(comp) that rigs any comp following the layer convention — generated, hand-built in AE, or imported & organized from Illustrator.

Hose IK

Each limb path is rewritten every frame to reach its controller and bow — drag a hand, the arm bends; the limb is straight art at rest.

Convention

Layers named CB_Arm_L, CB_Hand_L, etc.; generic art tagged CBART chest/head/.... Documented in docs/rig-convention.md.

Controllers

Master (move all), hips/chest pivots, and a hand/foot controller per limb — guide layers that never render.

Honest status

  • Working: arm + leg IK bending, hand/foot controllers, master move, full generated character rigs in one call.
  • Known bug: head-tilt control (the ring) currently misbehaves — face/head controls were always scoped as a later pass; the body rig is the load-bearing part and it's solid.
  • Also portable: because the rigger is art-agnostic, the same engine will rig your own hand-drawn or imported characters that follow the convention.
