What every Generate shuffles

Face

8 eyes (incl. star/heart), 4 brows, 5 mouths, 8 eyewear (often none).

Hair

6 hair + sleeve length (long / three-quarter / short) with cuffs, matched to the shirt color.

Wardrobe

5 palettes × solid/striped top × 5 body archetypes (petite, round, tall, lanky, average) — one clean silhouette, no more cape.

Finish

Spray-shade shadow + highlight per form, grain on top — the same texture system, applied automatically.

That’s already thousands of coherent combinations, before we add clothing variety and proportions.
